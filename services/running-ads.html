Platform Comparison

Meta Ads VS Google Ads

Choosing the right platform depends entirely on your business model, sales process, and market demand.

Demand Generation

Meta Advertising (FB & IG)

Best for disrupting potential buyers with visually engaging content and creating demand for new products or services.

Ideal For:
  • E-commerce products and visual brands
  • B2B and B2C direct lead generation
  • Building brand awareness and custom retargeting lists
Buying Intent: Passive / Interest-Based
Demand Capture

Google Search & Shopping

Best for capturing active buyers who are explicitly typing high-intent keywords searching for an immediate solution.

Ideal For:
  • High-urgency services (e.g., real estate, legal, emergency repairs)
  • Specific product searches and Google Shopping catalogues
  • High ticket local and commercial service businesses
Buying Intent: Active / Search-Based

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Obara Digital on your ad strategy.

How much ad spend budget do I need to start?

We recommend a minimum daily ad spend of ₦5,000 to ₦10,000 per platform to allow algorithm learning and generate sufficient data for optimization.

How quickly will I start seeing results?

Search ads can generate leads within 24–48 hours of launch. Meta campaigns typically require 3 to 7 days for algorithmic optimization and creative testing.

Do you create the ad visuals and video content?

Yes. We handle image graphics, video editing, headline copy, and campaign variations as part of our ongoing ad management process.
