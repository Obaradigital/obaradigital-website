Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Obara Digital use AI in advertising?

We use AI tools such as AnswerThePublic and BuzzSumo for deep audience research, topic mining, and intent analysis. Tools like Madgicx support campaign optimization and performance monitoring. Strategic decision-making and creative direction always remain under human oversight.

Do you guarantee sales or ROI?

No honest agency guarantees specific ROI or exact sales numbers, as business outcomes depend on multiple factors including product offer, landing page quality, market demand, and pricing. Our campaigns are designed to systematically improve acquisition efficiency and reach qualified prospects using proven data-driven practices.

Which platform is right for my business?

Google Ads works exceptionally well for capturing existing high-intent demand (prospects actively searching for solutions). Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok are ideal for visual discovery, demand generation, and precise interest-based audience targeting.

Are ad spend budgets included in management fees?

No. Your ad budget is paid directly to the ad platforms (Meta, Google, or TikTok). Our service fee covers research, campaign design, audience setup, creative testing management, signal configuration, tracking, and continuous human-led optimization.
