What Makes Ads Work

Your Budget Is Only One Part of the Equation

Spending more money does not automatically produce more customers. Successful advertising depends on the right strategy, audience, message, offer and conversion experience.

01

The Right Audience

Your ads need to reach people who are genuinely likely to need or want what your business offers. Better targeting can reduce wasted impressions and improve the quality of your leads.

02

A Strong Offer

Even well-targeted advertising can struggle when the offer is unclear or gives people no compelling reason to take the next step.

03

The Right Message

Your advertising needs to communicate the value of your product or service quickly and clearly enough to make the right prospect pay attention.

04

A Strong Conversion Experience

Getting someone to click is only the beginning. Your landing page, website, WhatsApp flow or enquiry process must make it easy for them to act.

05

Continuous Optimization

Campaign performance changes over time. Testing different audiences, creatives, keywords and messages helps identify what deserves more budget.

06

Accurate Tracking

Without reliable tracking, it becomes difficult to know which campaigns are generating enquiries, leads or sales and where your advertising budget is actually going.

We Focus on the Numbers That Matter

Impressions and clicks can be useful indicators, but your campaign should ultimately support a real business goal — whether that means more enquiries, calls, WhatsApp conversations, bookings or sales.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Running Ads?

Here are answers to some of the questions businesses ask before starting a paid advertising campaign.

How much should I spend on advertising?

There is no single budget that works for every business. Your starting budget should depend on your offer, target audience, location, competition, campaign objective and the amount of data needed to make informed decisions.

We can recommend a practical starting budget after understanding your business and advertising goals.

Do I pay you and the advertising platform separately?

Yes. Our advertising management fee is separate from your actual advertising budget.

Your advertising budget goes toward platforms such as Meta or Google, while our management fee covers strategy, campaign setup, monitoring, optimization and reporting.

Can you run both Facebook and Google Ads?

Yes. We can manage Meta Ads, including Facebook and Instagram advertising, as well as Google Ads.

Depending on your business, we can also recommend whether one platform or a combination of both makes more sense for your customer journey.

How quickly will I start getting results?

Advertising results vary from business to business. Factors such as your offer, market, competition, budget, landing page and campaign setup can all affect performance.

We do not promise a specific number of leads, sales or revenue. Instead, we monitor campaign data and make informed optimizations based on actual performance.

Do I need a website before running ads?

Not always. Some campaigns can send customers directly to WhatsApp, phone calls, lead forms or social media.

However, if your campaign depends on website conversions, your website or landing page needs to provide a clear, trustworthy and easy-to-use experience.

Can you help if I already have an advertising account?

Yes. We can review your existing campaigns, identify areas that may be limiting performance and recommend improvements.

Where appropriate, we can also help restructure campaigns instead of starting everything from scratch.
